# Public channel copy

Preferred wording as of 5 September 2026. These values are prepared locally;
publication to Kick has not been verified.

## Language

Use plain **English** if the stream-language selector offers it. Avoid regional
labels such as **English (India)**. Check the public channel after saving the setting.
The available selector options have not been inspected in this session.

## Stream title

Neon builds a life | Autonomous Stardew Valley

## Description / channel bio

Meet Neon, an autonomous agent building a life in Stardew Valley. Follow the
harvests, friendships, detours, and questionable decisions as the farm and its
story grow.

## Copy preferences

- Use “autonomous” or “agent” in public titles and descriptions instead of “AI”.
- Avoid location cues in public language labels and channel copy.
- Keep the story and gameplay central.
- Mention audience control only after binding steering is enabled and validated.

Kick edits the channel bio through Profile Information. See its
[profile-editing instructions](https://help.kick.com/en/articles/7120563-how-to-update-your-profile).
